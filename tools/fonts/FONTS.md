# Fonts for the DOCX template

Roboto Regular, Bold and Italic (TrueType), used by `tools/make-template.cjs` and embedded in the DOCX so the template
looks the same on computers without Roboto. Roboto is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org); the files come from Google Fonts and carry their copyright notice in their metadata.

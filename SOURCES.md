## Design references (ideas only)

References were studied for ideas only. Neither prototype copies their layouts, graphics or code.

| Reference | Observed | Idea taken forward |
|---|---|---|
| [Journal for Artistic Research](https://www.jar-online.net/en) | Text-first: a bold condensed wordmark, a vocabulary of disciplines, the current issue as an image strip, channels under ‘Network’. | Treat research contributions as first-class content that visitors can browse and filter, linking straight to the published work. |
| [Society for Artistic Research](https://societyforartisticresearch.org/) | A plain institutional layout: one strong statement, artwork beside it, a clear membership button, the next conference promoted in a band. | Keep one primary action (Join APARN) visible everywhere, and give the next conference its own band. |
| [SAR 2027 (Bratislava)](https://sar2027.eu/sk) | A strong typographic identity: dates and place first, a countdown, a clear call, text-size controls, a minimal cookie notice. | Lead every event with dates, place and a plain status; make the next step obvious. |
| [New Media Caucus, Header/Footer Gallery](https://newmediacaucus.org/header-footer-gallery) | A dark exhibition space that foregrounds curators, artists and work, with discovery through each show. | Put artists, titles and work in the foreground; let exhibition-like strips lead to the work itself. |
